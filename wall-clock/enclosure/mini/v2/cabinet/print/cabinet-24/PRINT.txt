mini-round-clock cabinet — 24-LED clock
=======================================
Black PETG. No supports on any part, and nothing on any part overhangs flatter
than 45 degrees by more than 3.5 mm. One file per part; copies as noted.

  mini-round-clock-cabinet-sleeve.stl          x1   BACK FACE DOWN
  mini-round-clock-cabinet-hatch.stl           x1   OUTSIDE FACE DOWN
  mini-round-clock-cabinet-drawer.stl          x1   OPEN SIDE UP
  mini-round-clock-cabinet-drawer-side-r1.stl  x1   OPEN SIDE UP
  mini-round-clock-cabinet-drawer-side-r2.stl  x1   OPEN SIDE UP
  mini-round-clock-cabinet-drawer-side-l1.stl  x1   OPEN SIDE UP

Every STL is already in its print orientation and sitting on z = 0. Drop them
on the plate as they are — do not let the slicer auto-orient them.

HOW THE CLOCK IS HELD — one bar, two screws
  It slides into a socket (five pads on the bore) and its face lands on a
  shoulder. One bar goes over the back of it, two M3 x 12 into the posts either
  side of 12 o'clock, and the pin on that bar drops into the back cover's
  keyhole — the wall hanger. That pin is what fixes the dial upright. Turn the
  clock a hair until the pin drops in, then tighten.

LASER (Glowforge, 3 mm plywood)
  mini-round-clock-cabinet-fronts.svg — all six fronts as ONE JOINED SHEET.
  They share their edges, so every line is cut once. Cut the holes first, then
  the lines across the sheet, then the outline LAST so the parts stay held.
  Each part comes out 0.10 mm smaller on a shared edge; the reveal is 0.80.
  MEASURE YOUR PLY FIRST. The files assume 3.00 mm. If yours differs, set PLY_T
  in params.py and run: python cabinet.py

BUY
  4  x M3 x 30 countersunk self-tapping   (the hatch)
  2  x M3 x 12 countersunk self-tapping   (the clock's retainer bar)
  10 x M3 x 12 pan head self-tapping      (the five pulls, two each)
  1 mm double-sided foam tape             (under the S3, one strip on the
                                           retainer bar, six squares on the
                                           face panel's stop tabs)

The clock goes in as you already print it: base + flange diffuser + back cover.
